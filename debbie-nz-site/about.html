How this site works

💗 The real bits

  • On the Pulse is a genuine, ongoing monthly read on data, measurement, privacy and AI. Every item is dated and links to its source.
  • The news roundups are AI-assisted, then read, questioned and approved by me.
  • My commentary (like "My take") is written by me, a human.
  • Comments, likes and the privacy policy are all real and working.

🧪 The test bench

  • Anything marked test is pretend: the shop and the sign-up form exist to test tracking.
  • No real orders, payments or sign-ups happen there, and nothing you enter is sent anywhere.
  • The tracking itself (GTM, GA4, consent) is real, which is the point: it's how I test my own tools.
