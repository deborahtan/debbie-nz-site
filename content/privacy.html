Privacy & cookies

What this site collects, why, and how to change your mind. Plain English, no surprises.

On this page In shortCookie choicesComments & likes AnalyticsWhat's storedYour rights

In short

  • No accounts, no ads, no selling or sharing your data.
  • Analytics stays off until you say yes, and you can change that any time below.
  • When you comment, your email is used only to check you're a real person. It's never stored, shown or shared.
  • IP addresses are never stored in full. Where one is needed to stop spam, it's scrambled (hashed) first and the scrambled version is deleted after 30 days.

Cookie choices (consent management)

This site uses Google Tag Manager with Google Consent Mode. Before you choose, every optional category is off. Your choice is saved in your own browser for 12 months, then you'll be asked again.

Essential

Makes the site work: remembering your cookie choice, likes, spam protection, and Cloudflare Turnstile tokens. Always on.

Always on

What happens when you say no

Google's tags don't set analytics or advertising cookies. With Consent Mode, they may still send a basic, cookieless signal (for example, that a page was viewed) without identifiers, which Google uses for aggregate modelling.

Comments & likes

Comments

  • Your email: checked to make sure it's a real address from a mainstream provider (Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, Yahoo, Proton, Xtra and similar), then thrown away. It isn't saved anywhere, shown to anyone or sent to analytics.
  • What's saved: your comment, the time you posted it, and a friendly nickname (like Sunny Kererū) made from a random code in your browser, not from anything about you.
  • Spam protection: a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address, used to limit how fast comments can be posted. It can't be turned back into your IP, and it's deleted after 30 days.
  • Comments are public as soon as they're posted. I may remove anything abusive, off-topic or spammy.

Likes

A random code saved in your browser lets you undo a like. It isn't linked to who you are. The same 30-day scrambled-IP rule applies.

Analytics

With your consent, Google Analytics 4 collects things like pages viewed, how far you scroll, which posts you open, likes, shares and comments posted (never the comment text or your email), search terms (site search only), and errors. Google Analytics 4 doesn't log or store IP addresses. Please don't type personal details into site search.

Your rights

Under New Zealand's Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see or correct personal information held about you. Because comments aren't linked to an email or account, tell me which comment (the edition, date and wording) if you'd like it removed. Your visit nickname is generated in the browser and cannot be linked back to you. If you're not happy with how I've handled a privacy request, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.
